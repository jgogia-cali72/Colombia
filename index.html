Day 6 · Canyoning & Guatapé Town

Thursday, October 29, 2026
9:30 AM – 1:30 PM
🧗 Koora Canyoning — Guatapé River Canyon
~4 km down the river: ziplining, rappelling, cliff jumps from different heights, natural water slides and swimming in pools. 20-min jungle hike in (watch for grey titi monkeys), ~3 hrs in the river. Difficulty 3.5/5. Koora confirmed all three kids can go.

Included: wetsuit, helmet and all technical gear, 2 certified guides (first aid + water rescue), GoPro photos/videos, medical insurance, water and snacks.
Bring: swimsuits to wear under the wetsuit, lace-up shoes that can get soaked (no sandals), sunscreen, and a bag of dry clothes (kept at the start). Leave phones and valuables behind — everything gets wet.
Price: COP 300,000/person with transport from Guatapé's main square (~COP 1,500,000 for 5), or COP 250,000/person without.
📍 Meeting point (with transport): Koora office, Guatapé · alt. link · Self-drive start point: here
